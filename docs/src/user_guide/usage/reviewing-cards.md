# Reviewing Cards

### Getting Started

When you first integrate PrioritySieve you will see lots of trivial expressions. Tag them as known, keep moving, and run
[Recalc](recalc.md) frequently so the entry database stays fresh. The first few dozen reviewed entries unlock the real
benefits—after that, the add-on reliably surfaces useful material.

### Marking Entries as Reviewed

If you already understand the card you are shown, press `K` (“Known”). That adds the `am-known-manually` tag so the entry
will be treated as reviewed the next time you run Recalc. Cards for reviewed entries are automatically suspended while
duplicates are parked for later study.

### Encountering Difficult Cards

Sometimes a card will still feel out of reach. Suspend it, delete it, or replace it with a better example—PrioritySieve is
most effective when you spend time on low-friction input.

### Finding a Better Card

Press `L` to open the browser and see all cards that share the current entry. `Shift+L` shows *every* card, even those you
have already reviewed. Right-click and choose **Learn Card Now** to bring a different card to the front of the new queue.

### Let PrioritySieve Handle Duplicates

During Recalc, PrioritySieve automatically suspends lower-priority cards that share an entry and can optionally suspend
entries missing from your priority lists. There is no longer any manual “skip” workflow to manage.
